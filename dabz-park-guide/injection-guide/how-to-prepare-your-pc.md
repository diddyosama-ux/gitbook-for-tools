#### <mark style="color:blue;">**Configure Your Game Settings**</mark>

After the Fixer Tool is completed, start your game. **Do not inject yet.**

Once you're in-game, make sure you are running in **Fullscreen or Borderless** mode. **DLSS should be turned off**, and your graphics settings should be set to **Medium or Low**, unless you have a high-end PC.

After configuring your game settings, **close the game**.
