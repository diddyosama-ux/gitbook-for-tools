# Steel Series Overlay

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Download & install SteelSeries Sonar from the link below.

SteelSeries Sonar Download: [https://steelseries.com/gg/downloads/gg/latest/windows](https://steelseries.com/gg/downloads/gg/latest/windows)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Once you install & open the app, create an account or login if you already have an account

Click on the Sonar tab as seen below, for first time users you will need to setup your inputs/outputs. You can continue to the next step once you see the screen below & Sonar is enabled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (75).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Click on the Moments tab as seen below

for first time users you will have a prompt you will need to go through. You can continue to the next step once you see the screen below & Moments is enabled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (76).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Now enter the settings part of the app

click on the Notifications tab under General. Ensure both options available are disabled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (77).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Now go to the Overlays and Notifications tab under Engine

. Ensure both options available are disabled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (78).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Now go to the Overlays and Notifications tab under Moments

Ensure the 3 options available are disabled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (79).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Now go to the Shortcuts tab under Sonar

Click Master, then set “Master - Volume Up” to a key such as F12 as seen below. This is what is referred to as your Sonar Shortcut Key in the guide, it is what initializes the overlay for the cheat to utilize.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (80).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Now go to the Overlays tab under Sonar.&#x20;

Ensure the top option is enabled and the bottom option is disabled as seen below.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (81).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Final Finish

That’s it, your SteelSeries Sonar Overlay is now setup for your cheat! Now just follow the guide for the specific cheat you are using and you will see when you need to press your Sonar Shortcut key.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
